Why membership

What membership actually changes.

  • Consistency is the active ingredient

    Almost every therapy in the studio works cumulatively. One sauna is pleasant. Two a week for three months is a different thing entirely, and membership is how people actually get there.

  • One plan across both sides of the studio

    Your recovery work and your chiropractic care are coordinated rather than scheduled separately by two people who never speak. That is the reason both live under one roof.

  • Booking you can rely on

    Members get a priority window before appointments open generally, which matters most for evening and Saturday slots.

  • Room to bring someone

    Guest passes at the member rate, because the people who benefit most are usually the ones being dragged in by someone else.

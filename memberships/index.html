How credits work

Each credit may be used toward one eligible Reset service. If you enjoy more than one service during the same studio visit, each service uses a separate credit. For example, a red light session followed by compression during the same visit would use two credits.

  • RESET 4 — 4 service credits per month
  • RESET 8 — 8 service credits per month
  • RESET 16 — 16 service credits per month

All three tiers require a six-month commitment. Service credits cover the wellness and recovery menu; unused credits roll over for thirty days and no further. RESET 16 is the high-frequency option and is not unlimited. Members may bring a guest to eligible private-room services at member guest pricing. Chiropractic care is separate clinical care provided by Dr. Candy, LLC and is booked on its own path — it is not included in any membership.

Why membership

What membership actually changes.

  • Consistency is the active ingredient

    Almost every therapy in the studio works cumulatively. One sauna is pleasant. Two a week for six months is a different thing entirely, and the six-month commitment is what actually gets people there.

  • Everything works better together

    Heat, cold, light, pressure and sound complement each other, and a membership is what makes using more than one of them realistic. Chiropractic care is separate clinical care on its own booking path, not part of a membership.

  • Booking you can rely on

    Members get a priority window before appointments open generally, which matters most for evening and Saturday slots.

  • Room to bring someone

    Member guest pricing on eligible private-room services, because the people who benefit most are usually the ones being dragged in by someone else.

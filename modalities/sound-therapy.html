Vibroacoustic Sound Therapy

Low-frequency sound you feel as much as hear.

Music and low-frequency vibration delivered through the body rather than only through the ears, used to guide the nervous system into a slower, more restorative state.

What it is

Vibroacoustic therapy transmits low-frequency sound through a table into the body. You hear the music, and you also feel it, which is a strange and quite specific sensation for about ninety seconds before it becomes deeply comfortable.

The intent is entrainment. Slow, steady, predictable input gives an overstimulated nervous system something simple to follow, and it tends to follow it downward.

What it supports

  • A direct route into parasympathetic rest
  • Useful for people who cannot meditate and have stopped trying
  • Frequently reported improvement in sleep that night
  • No effort required from you at all

Commonly booked for

  • Nervous system regulation
  • Sleep difficulty and racing thoughts
  • Burnout recovery
  • Anyone who finds stillness hard

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Lie down

    Fully clothed, on your back, blanket if you want one. Lights come down.

  2. The programme

    A composed session of frequency and music. You are not being asked to focus on anything.

  3. A slow exit

    We bring the room back up gradually. Do not schedule anything urgent afterwards.

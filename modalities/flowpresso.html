Flowpresso® Lymphatic Compression Therapy

Compression, heat and deep pressure in one garment.

A three-in-one therapy that combines sequential compression, far infrared heat and deep pressure across the whole body. Most clients describe it as the deepest rest they have had in months.

What it is

Flowpresso wraps the body in a garment that applies gentle sequential compression, far infrared warmth and sustained deep pressure at the same time. The combination is aimed at the lymphatic system, which has no pump of its own and relies on movement and pressure to circulate.

The deep pressure component is why people fall asleep in this one. Broad, even, sustained pressure across the body is a well-documented route into a parasympathetic state, which is the same principle behind a weighted blanket, applied with far more precision.

What it supports

  • Supports lymphatic movement and fluid balance
  • Deep pressure that reliably shifts the nervous system toward rest
  • A common favourite for clients managing swelling or heaviness
  • You do nothing for forty-five minutes, which is the point

Commonly booked for

  • Lymphatic health and fluid retention
  • Perimenopause and menopause support
  • Burnout and sleep difficulty
  • Anyone who struggles to switch off

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Dressed and wrapped

    You stay fully clothed in comfortable layers. The garment covers legs, abdomen and arms.

  2. Three systems at once

    Compression cycles, warmth builds, and pressure holds steady. Nothing is sharp or sudden.

  3. Coming back

    We leave time at the end because people genuinely need a minute. Water and a quiet chair.

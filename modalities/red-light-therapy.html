Red Light Therapy

Red and near-infrared light at therapeutic wavelengths.

Full-body exposure to specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light, studied for their effect on cellular energy production, skin and tissue recovery.

Session
20 minutes
Category
Wellness & Recovery
Book Red Light Therapy

What it is

Red light therapy, sometimes called photobiomodulation, delivers light at wavelengths that tissue absorbs rather than reflects. The research interest centres on mitochondria and the cellular energy they produce.

It is one of the least dramatic things we offer. You stand in front of a panel, it is warm and bright, and twenty minutes later you leave. The effects are cumulative and show up over weeks of consistent sessions rather than in a single visit.

What it supports

  • Studied for skin tone, texture and collagen support
  • May support muscle recovery and tissue repair
  • No downtime and no post-session sensitivity
  • Stacks easily onto any other service

Commonly booked for

  • Skin health and healthy aging
  • Training recovery
  • Adding to a sauna or plunge visit
  • Consistency-minded clients

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Twenty minutes, standing

    Protective eyewear provided. You rotate partway through so exposure is even.

  2. Skin exposed

    Light needs to reach tissue, so the more skin exposed the better. Private room, your comfort level.

  3. Straight back to your day

    No cool-down needed. This is the service people book on a lunch break.

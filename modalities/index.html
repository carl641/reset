The Modalities

Every path leads to the same place.

Heat, cold, light, pressure, touch — each one is simply a different way of helping your body remember what calm feels like.

Contrast Therapy

Heat and cold in deliberate sequence.

60 minutes

Infrared Sauna

Deep, dry heat at a temperature you can stay in.

40 minutes

Cold Plunge

Short, controlled cold. Guided every time.

20 minutes

Red Light Therapy

Red and near-infrared light at therapeutic wavelengths.

20 minutes

Flowpresso® Therapy

Compression, heat and deep pressure in one garment.

45 minutes

NormaTec® Compression

Dynamic compression for legs, hips and arms.

30 minutes

Sound Therapy

Low-frequency sound you feel as much as hear.

30 minutes

Neuro Light Therapy

Patterned light and sound for focus and calm.

25 minutes

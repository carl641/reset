Your first visit

Nobody gets thrown in at the deep end.

Most people arrive having never done any of this properly. Your first visit is built around that, and the pacing is set by you rather than by a protocol.

What to expect

How the visit goes.

  1. The first fifteen minutes

    Arrival and intake

    Health history and a short conversation about what you are actually trying to change. This is where we find out about conditions, medications and anything that affects whether heat, cold or compression are appropriate for you.

  2. Before anything starts

    A walk through the space

    You see the rooms, the plunge, the sauna and where everything lives before you are asked to use any of it. Nobody is put into cold water having never seen it.

  3. The session

    Guided, and paced by you

    First sessions are staffed. Someone walks you through the breathing, sets the timer where you can see it, and stays in the room. You can stop at any point and it will not be treated as a failure.

  4. Afterwards

    A proper landing

    Time to rewarm, sit and drink something before you leave. We will also tell you honestly what we think a sensible next visit looks like, which is sometimes less than you expected.

Before you come in

Preparing for your visit

None of this is complicated, and the studio has spares of most things. The one section worth reading properly is the last one.

Bring

  • Swimwear for sauna, plunge or contrast therapy
  • Loose, comfortable clothing for compression, sound therapy and chiropractic care
  • Towels, water and toiletries are provided

Timing

  • Arrive fifteen minutes early on your first visit
  • Ten minutes is plenty after that
  • Leave a buffer afterwards, especially for sound therapy and Flowpresso

Food and water

  • Something light one to two hours before
  • Hydrate across the day rather than all at once beforehand
  • Skip alcohol before any heat or cold session

Tell us about

  • Pregnancy, cardiovascular conditions or high blood pressure
  • Recent surgery, injuries or implanted devices
  • Any medication that affects heat tolerance or blood pressure

If you are managing an ongoing condition, are pregnant, or are unsure whether any of this is appropriate for you, speak with your healthcare provider first. We are glad to talk to them about what a session involves. Chiropractic care is provided by Dr. Candy, LLC and begins with a full consultation.

Questions

Questions people ask before booking

Before you arrive

Do I need any experience with cold or sauna?

No. Most people arrive having never done either properly. Your first visit includes a walkthrough of the space and the technique, and the pacing is set by you. Nobody is timed against anyone else.

What should I bring?

Swimwear if you are using the sauna, plunge or contrast therapy. Comfortable loose clothing for compression, sound therapy and chiropractic care. Towels, water and toiletries are provided.

How early should I get there?

Fifteen minutes before your first appointment so you have time to complete intake forms and be shown around without rushing. Ten minutes is plenty after that.

Should I eat beforehand?

Something light an hour or two before is ideal. A heavy meal immediately before heat or cold is uncomfortable, and arriving completely empty is worse. Hydrate properly during the day rather than drinking a litre in the car park.

During your session

How cold is the plunge, and how long do I have to stay in?

The plunge is held in the range most of the research uses, and exposures are short by design. Two to three minutes is a full session for most people, and less than that on a first visit is completely normal. You can get out at any point.

Will someone be with me?

For cold plunge and contrast therapy, yes. Someone guides your first sessions and stays in the room. Sauna, compression, light and sound therapies are private, with a member of the team nearby.

Can I combine services in one visit?

That is how most people use the studio. Sauna into plunge, compression after a chiropractic visit, red light onto the end of anything. We will help you sequence it so the order actually makes sense.

What if I do not like it?

Tell us and we will stop. Every service can be ended at any point, and there is no version of this where you are talked into staying in cold water you want to get out of.

Chiropractic care

Who provides chiropractic care at The Reset Studio?

Chiropractic services are provided by Dr. Candy Ellis through Dr. Candy, LLC, an independent professional practice operating within the studio. You book through us and experience one visit, and the clinical care is delivered under her licence.

Do I need a chiropractic consultation before being adjusted?

Yes. Every chiropractic relationship starts with a full consultation covering history, movement assessment and orthopaedic testing. Treatment follows an assessment, never precedes it.

Do you take insurance?

Insurance participation is confirmed at the time of booking. Contact us with your plan details before your first appointment and we will tell you exactly what applies.

What if chiropractic is not the right answer for my problem?

You will be told, and referred. An honest assessment that sends you elsewhere is a better outcome than a course of care that was never going to help.

Health and safety

Are there conditions that would rule these services out?

Some, yes. Pregnancy, cardiovascular conditions, uncontrolled blood pressure, certain medications, recent surgery and a small number of other circumstances affect whether heat, cold or compression are appropriate. Our intake form asks about all of it, and we will talk it through with you rather than leaving you to guess.

Should I check with my doctor first?

If you are managing an ongoing medical condition, are pregnant, or are unsure, yes. We are glad to speak with your provider about what a session involves.

How is the plunge water kept clean?

Continuous filtration and sanitation, with water quality tested and logged daily. The plunge is a shared amenity and is treated with the standards that implies.

Is there an age minimum?

Eighteen for independent booking. Younger clients can be seen with a parent or guardian present and, where relevant, appropriate clinical clearance.

Booking and memberships

How do I book?

Online through the booking link on any page, or by calling the studio. First visits are best booked by phone so we can put you in the right service.

What is your cancellation policy?

Twenty-four hours notice to change or cancel without charge. Late cancellations and missed appointments are charged in full, which is what keeps prime slots available to people who want them.

Can I pause a membership?

Yes. Memberships can be paused for a defined period for travel, illness or injury. Ask at the desk or by email and we will handle it.

Do sessions expire?

Membership sessions roll over for thirty days. Package sessions have a longer window, confirmed at purchase.

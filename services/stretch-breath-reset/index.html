What it is

A fifteen-minute guided session pairing gentle guided stretching with breathwork. Someone is with you for the whole of it, setting the pace and the sequence around what your body will actually do that day.

This is a standalone guided service, not an add-on. Book it on its own when fifteen minutes is what you have, or put it alongside another service when you want to arrive settled or leave loosened.

What it supports

  • Supports mobility through the hips, back and shoulders
  • Designed for relaxation
  • May help steady your breathing
  • Commonly used for finding a calmer state

Commonly booked for

  • Tension and stress
  • A quick reset on its own
  • Between other services
  • Short lunch breaks

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. A short conversation

    What is tight, what is sore, and what you want out of the next quarter of an hour.

  2. A guided sequence

    Stretches and breathing paced by you. Nothing is forced and nothing is held past comfortable.

  3. Leave loosened and settled

    Fifteen minutes end to end, so it fits either side of the rest of your day.

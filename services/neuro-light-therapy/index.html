An optional enhancement to Zenthesia Vibroacoustic.

What it is

Neurological light is an optional enhancement to Zenthesia Vibroacoustic, not a service you book on its own. Patterned light is delivered at specific frequencies over the session you are already lying in, and with your eyes closed it reads as color, shape and movement that is not on any screen.

Different settings are aimed at different states. There is one for focus, one for calm and one aimed at sleep, and we will ask which you are after before we start. Brightness is adjustable throughout and it can be turned off at any point without ending your Zenthesia session.

What it supports

  • Settings aimed at focus, calm or sleep
  • Designed as a structured way into meditative states
  • Added to a Zenthesia session rather than booked separately
  • Genuinely enjoyable, which matters for consistency

Commonly booked for

  • Focus and mental clarity
  • Stress and overstimulation
  • Sleep onset difficulty
  • Curious first-timers

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Ask for it when you book Zenthesia

    Tell us what you want out of the session and we will set the light to match.

  2. Eyes closed, through the session

    The light runs alongside the vibroacoustic table. Brightness is adjustable and it can be turned off at any point.

  3. Reorient

    We bring the room back up gradually, the same as any Zenthesia session.

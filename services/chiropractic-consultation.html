What it is

Every chiropractic relationship here starts with a consultation. Dr. Candy takes a full history, assesses how you move, and runs the orthopedic and neurological testing needed to understand what is actually driving your symptoms.

You leave with an explanation in plain language and a recommended plan, including an honest view of what chiropractic care will and will not help with. If something needs a referral, you will be told.

What it supports

  • A clear explanation of what is happening and why
  • A treatment plan with a defined shape and endpoint
  • Referral when another provider is the right answer
  • Integrates directly with your recovery services

Commonly booked for

  • Persistent back, neck or joint pain
  • Recurring headaches
  • Reduced mobility
  • Anyone starting chiropractic care

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. History

    What hurts, how long, what makes it worse, what you have already tried. This part takes real time.

  2. Assessment

    Movement screening, orthopedic and neurological testing, and postural assessment.

  3. The plan

    Findings explained in language you can repeat to someone else, and a recommended course of care.

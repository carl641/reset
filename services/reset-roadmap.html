What it is

Not sure where to start? Meet one-on-one with Dr. Candy to talk through what you want from Reset — recovery, stress support, sleep, energy, or simply feeling more like yourself — and leave with a practical starting plan.

This is a wellness consultation, separate from chiropractic and clinical care. It books through the wellness flow rather than the chiropractic path, and it is not an assessment, a diagnosis or a course of treatment. If what you need is clinical, Dr. Candy will tell you and point you at the chiropractic consultation instead.

What it supports

  • Choosing the services that fit what you are after
  • Building a plan you can actually keep to
  • A clear starting point instead of a guess
  • Designed for anyone new to the studio

Commonly booked for

  • First-timers
  • Anyone unsure which services fit
  • Building a routine
  • Coming back after a long gap

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. What you are after

    Recovery, stress support, sleep, energy, or simply feeling more like yourself. We start with the goal rather than the equipment.

  2. What fits

    A walk through the services that suit it, honestly including the ones that do not, and what a sensible frequency looks like.

  3. Your starting plan

    You leave with a written starting point — what to book first, and what to add once the first thing is a habit.

In One Visit

Most visits are more than one thing.

Three combinations that come up constantly. Tell us what you are after and we will sequence it properly.

  • Train and recover

    Chiropractic adjustment, NormaTec compression, red light.

    About 75 minutes · Training blocks and anyone managing load.

    Infrared sauna, then Flowpresso, then home.

    About 90 minutes · Sleep, burnout and evenings that need to end differently.

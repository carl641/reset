In One Visit

Most visits are more than one thing.

Three combinations that come up constantly, all within wellness and recovery. Tell us what you are after and we will sequence it properly.

  • The full reset

    Contrast therapy, then compression, then Zenthesia.

    About 2 hours · A first visit, or a reset after a heavy month.

    About 65 minutes · Training blocks and anyone managing load.

    About 70 minutes · Sleep, burnout and evenings that need to end differently.

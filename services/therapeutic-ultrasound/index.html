Therapeutic Ultrasound

Sound waves into deep soft tissue.

High-frequency sound delivered through a handheld head to generate deep tissue warming and support healing in tendons, ligaments and muscle.

Book Therapeutic Ultrasound

What it is

Therapeutic ultrasound sends high-frequency sound waves into soft tissue, producing warming at a depth that a heat pack cannot reach. It is used to improve tissue extensibility before manual work and to support healing in slower-recovering structures.

Like electrical stimulation, this is a supporting service. It is used when the tissue involved makes it the right tool, not as a default add-on.

What it supports

  • Deep tissue warming beyond the reach of surface heat
  • Supports healing in tendon and ligament injuries
  • Improves tissue readiness for hands-on treatment
  • Entirely painless

Commonly booked for

  • Tendon and ligament injuries
  • Scar tissue and adhesions
  • Chronic soft tissue complaints
  • Preparation for manual therapy

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Gel applied

    A conductive gel lets the sound head make proper contact.

  2. Slow, continuous movement

    The head is kept moving over the area. You may feel mild warmth, or nothing at all.

  3. Straight into treatment

    Usually followed immediately by manual therapy while the tissue is warm.

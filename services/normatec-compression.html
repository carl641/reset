NormaTec® Compression Therapy

Dynamic compression for legs, hips and arms.

Pneumatic compression boots that use patterned pulsing to move fluid out of the limbs. The recovery tool professional athletes have used for years, available by the session.

Book NormaTec Compression

What it is

NormaTec boots inflate in a patterned sequence from the feet upward, holding and releasing in a rhythm designed to move fluid toward the core rather than simply squeezing.

This is the most straightforwardly practical service in the studio. If you have trained hard, stood all day, flown recently or spent a week in shoes you regret, thirty minutes in the boots does something you can feel immediately.

What it supports

  • Moves fluid out of tired or swollen limbs
  • May reduce post-training soreness
  • Immediate, obvious relief for heavy legs
  • Easy to add to the end of any visit

Commonly booked for

  • Athletic recovery and training blocks
  • Heavy or swollen legs
  • Travel recovery
  • People on their feet all day

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Boots on, seat back

    You stay clothed. Legs are standard, with arm and hip attachments available.

  2. Your pressure

    Intensity is adjustable throughout. Firm should feel good, never sharp.

  3. Thirty minutes

    Long enough to matter, short enough to book on the way home.

What it is

Zenthesia is vibroacoustic therapy: low-frequency sound transmitted through a table into the body. You hear the music, and you also feel it, which is a strange and quite specific sensation for about ninety seconds before it becomes deeply comfortable.

The intent is entrainment. Slow, steady, predictable input gives an overstimulated nervous system something simple to follow, and it tends to follow it downward.

What it supports

  • A direct route into parasympathetic rest
  • Useful for people who cannot meditate and have stopped trying
  • Frequently reported improvement in sleep that night
  • No effort required from you at all

Commonly booked for

  • Nervous system regulation
  • Sleep difficulty and racing thoughts
  • Burnout recovery
  • Anyone who finds stillness hard

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Lie down

    Fully clothed, on your back, blanket if you want one. Lights come down.

  2. The program

    A composed session of frequency and music. You are not being asked to focus on anything.

  3. A slow exit

    We bring the room back up gradually. Do not schedule anything urgent afterwards.

Optional enhancement

Neurological light. Zenthesia can be run with an optional neurological-light enhancement: rhythmic, patterned light at specific frequencies, layered over the session you are already lying in. With your eyes closed it reads as color, shape and movement, and it gives an overstimulated mind something simple to follow.

Different settings are aimed at different states — one for focus, one for calm, one for sleep — and we will ask which you are after before we start. Brightness is adjustable throughout and it can be turned off at any point without ending the session. It is an enhancement to Zenthesia rather than a service you book on its own.

More about the light enhancement

What it supports

  • Settings aimed at focus, calm or sleep
  • Designed as a structured way into meditative states
  • Commonly used for stress and overstimulation
  • Added to a Zenthesia session, not booked separately

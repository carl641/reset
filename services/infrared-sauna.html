Infrared Sauna

Deep, dry heat at a temperature you can stay in.

Full-spectrum infrared warms the body directly rather than heating the air around it, so sessions are longer, gentler and easier to tolerate than a traditional sauna.

Book Infrared Sauna

What it is

A traditional sauna heats the room and the room heats you. Infrared skips the middle step and warms tissue directly, which means a session at a far more comfortable air temperature still raises your core temperature meaningfully.

The practical difference is time. Most people cannot sit in a 190 degree room for forty minutes. They can sit in ours, and the longer, steadier exposure is what makes the session feel restorative rather than endured.

What it supports

  • Passive cardiovascular load without joint impact
  • Supports muscle relaxation and recovery
  • Regular sauna use is associated with better sleep quality
  • A genuinely quiet forty minutes, which is its own therapy

Commonly booked for

  • Sleep and evening wind-down
  • Muscle tension and stiffness
  • Anyone who finds traditional saunas too aggressive
  • Recovery days

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Your room, your settings

    Private cabin, chromotherapy lighting, and a temperature you can adjust. Bring headphones or take the silence.

  2. Forty minutes

    Long enough for a real thermal load, short enough to fit into a lunch break. Water is provided and we will remind you to use it.

  3. Cool down

    A quiet space to sit afterwards so you are not walking straight from heat into traffic.

What it is

A traditional sauna heats the room and the room heats you. Infrared skips the middle step and warms tissue directly, which means a session at a far more comfortable air temperature still raises your core temperature meaningfully.

The practical difference is tolerance. Most people cannot sit in a 190 degree room for half an hour. They can sit in ours, and the steadier, more comfortable exposure is what makes the session feel restorative rather than endured.

What it supports

  • Passive cardiovascular load without joint impact
  • Supports muscle relaxation and recovery
  • Regular sauna use is associated with better sleep quality
  • A genuinely quiet half hour, which is its own therapy

Commonly booked for

  • Sleep and evening wind-down
  • Muscle tension and stiffness
  • Anyone who finds traditional saunas too aggressive
  • Recovery days

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Your cabin, your settings

    Chromotherapy lighting and a temperature you can adjust. Bring headphones or take the silence. The cabin is yours for the session — on your own, or with the guests you booked in with.

  2. Thirty minutes

    Long enough for a real thermal load, short enough to fit into a lunch break. Water is provided and we will remind you to use it.

  3. Cool down

    A quiet space to sit afterwards so you are not walking straight from heat into traffic.

  4. Or bring someone

    The sauna seats up to four, so it can be booked Together with guests you have chosen to come in with. We never put strangers in a cabin together.

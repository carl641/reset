Neurological Light Therapy

Patterned light and sound for focus and calm.

Rhythmic light and audio delivered through a visor and headphones, using patterned frequency to guide brain states toward focus, calm or sleep depending on the programme.

Session
25 minutes
Category
Wellness & Recovery
Book Neuro Light Therapy

What it is

This one surprises people. You wear a visor and headphones, and patterned light and sound are delivered at specific frequencies. With your eyes closed you see colour, shape and movement that is not on any screen.

Different programmes target different states. There is one for focus, one for calm and one aimed at sleep. It is the closest thing in the studio to a guided experience without anyone talking you through it.

What it supports

  • Programmes targeted at focus, calm or sleep
  • A structured way into meditative states
  • Short enough to use regularly
  • Genuinely enjoyable, which matters for consistency

Commonly booked for

  • Focus and mental clarity
  • Stress and overstimulation
  • Sleep onset difficulty
  • Curious first-timers

The Session

What the session is like.

  1. Choose a programme

    We will ask what you want out of the session and pick accordingly.

  2. Eyes closed, twenty-five minutes

    Reclined in a private room. Brightness is adjustable and you can stop at any point.

  3. Reorient

    A few minutes of ordinary light before you head out.

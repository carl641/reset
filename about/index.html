About the studio

Built for the part of health that everyone skips.

Most people know what they should be doing. Very few have anywhere to do it properly, with equipment that works and a space built for actually using it. That gap is the whole business.

The story

The Reset Studio began with a simple idea: taking care of yourself shouldn’t feel like another thing on your to-do list.

After years in clinical practice, Dr. Candy Ellis kept seeing the same thing. People wanted to feel better and take better care of themselves, but recovery was often the first thing to disappear when life got busy.

And life is busy.

We work. We take care of families. We build careers and businesses. We carry responsibilities, schedules, stress and people we love. Somewhere in the middle of all of that, it’s easy to lose the feeling of being fully ourselves.

Candy understood that personally, too.

She imagined a place where caring for your body and nervous system could become a normal part of life—not something reserved for vacations, spa days, or the point when you’re already depleted.

That idea became The Reset Studio: a thoughtfully designed space bringing together recovery and wellness experiences in one beautiful, approachable place.

But Reset was never really about the equipment.

It’s about how you feel when you leave—a little quieter, a little lighter, more grounded and more capable of meeting whatever is waiting for you outside these doors.

Because sometimes a reset isn’t about becoming a new version of yourself.

It’s about finding your way back to yourself.

Why we exist

Because the people who most need to recover are often the least likely to make time for it.

Our clients are busy, capable people with full lives. They take care of families, build careers, run businesses and show up for the people who depend on them. Making space for themselves often comes last.

The Reset Studio makes recovery easier to return to—one beautiful space, thoughtfully chosen experiences, and time intentionally set aside to reset.

Reset your body. Reset your mind. Return to yourself.

Our Philosophy

What we actually believe.

  1. Evidence first, and honest about its limits

    Everything we offer is chosen intentionally. We look at the research, stay honest about its limitations, and distinguish between what is well established, what is promising, and what is still emerging.

  2. Regulation, as well as relaxation

    Feeling calm for an hour matters. We also believe recovery should support the way you feel beyond your session—creating more space, resilience and capacity for the rest of your life.

  3. Consistency beats intensity

    The occasional reset feels good. Making recovery part of your life can mean more. We believe consistency matters more than chasing one extraordinary session—which is why Reset is designed to be a place you actually want to come back to.

  4. You set the pace

    Your experience belongs to you. Stay longer when it feels right. Stop when you’ve had enough. Try something new when you’re ready. We’ll guide you, answer your questions and help you find what works for you—but we won’t push you.

  5. Better together

    Heat, cold, light, compression, sound and breath each offer something different. Bringing them together in one place gives you the freedom to discover what feels right for you—and makes recovery easier to build into real life.

Dr. Candy Ellis

Founder + Chiropractic Physician

Dr. Candy Ellis has been a chiropractor since 2012, but her interest in wellness has always extended beyond what happens in the treatment room.

Years of caring for patients taught her that feeling better is only part of the equation. The harder part is creating enough space for recovery to become a consistent part of real life.

As a doctor, wife, mom and business owner, Candy understands that personally. The Reset Studio grew from her desire to create the kind of place she wanted for herself and her patients—a place where caring for your body and nervous system feels approachable, enjoyable and easy to return to.

Her philosophy is simple: wellness shouldn’t take over your life. It should help you live it.

Chiropractic services are provided by Dr. Candy, LLC, an independent professional practice operating within The Reset Studio. Chiropractic care is separate from The Reset Studio’s wellness and recovery services and is not included in studio memberships.

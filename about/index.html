About the studio

Built for the part of health that everyone skips.

Most people know what they should be doing. Very few have anywhere to do it properly, with equipment that works and someone who knows how to sequence it. That gap is the whole business.

The story

The Reset Studio started as a frustration rather than a business plan. Dr. Candy Ellis spent years treating people whose recovery was scattered across four buildings: a chiropractor here, a gym with a broken sauna there, a massage therapist who had never spoken to either.

The clinical work was going well. What was not going well was everything around it. A patient would leave an adjustment that had gone perfectly, go back to a life with no recovery in it at all, and be back in six weeks with the same complaint. The treatment was not the limiting factor. The conditions around it were.

So the studio was designed backwards from that problem. One space, one schedule, one plan. Recovery equipment that is genuinely good rather than a token amenity, clinical care in the same building, and a team who talk to each other about the same person.

The result does not look like a clinic and it does not behave like a spa. It is closer to a training facility for the systems nobody trains.

Why we exist

Because the people who most need to recover are the least likely to make time for it, and because telling them to relax has never once worked.

The clients who walk in here are usually competent, busy and running a deficit they have decided to treat as normal. They are not going to fix it with a candle and an intention. They will fix it with a scheduled appointment, equipment that does something measurable, and someone expecting them on Thursday.

Reset your body. Reset your mind. Return to yourself.

Our Philosophy

What we actually believe.

  1. Evidence first, and honest about its limits

    Every therapy here has research behind it. That research is not always as strong or as settled as the wellness industry implies, and we will tell you where the evidence is good, where it is promising, and where it is mostly enthusiasm.

  2. Regulation over relaxation

    Feeling calm for an hour is easy to sell and not very useful. We are working on your capacity to return to calm on your own, which is a skill that transfers to the rest of your week.

  3. Consistency beats intensity

    Almost nothing here works as a one-off. We would rather you did two sessions a week for six months than one extraordinary session you talk about for a year.

  4. You set the pace

    Nobody is talked into another round or another minute. If you want to get out, you get out, and that is not a failed session.

  5. One plan, not five providers

    Your recovery work and your clinical care are planned together by people who speak to each other. That coordination is the reason the studio exists in this form.

Dr. Candy Ellis

Founder and Chiropractic Physician

Dr. Ellis is a chiropractic physician who came to recovery work through the back door. Years of treating pain taught her that the adjustment was rarely the hard part. Getting someone into a state where their body could actually hold the change was.

Her clinical approach is assessment-led and conservative. Treatment follows a diagnosis, plans have a defined shape and an endpoint, and if chiropractic care is not the right answer for your problem you will be told and referred rather than kept on the schedule.

Chiropractic services are provided by Dr. Candy, LLC, an independent professional practice operating within The Reset Studio. It is a legal and clinical distinction that matters on paper. For you it is one visit, one schedule and one plan.
